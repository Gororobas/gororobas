# GBIF evidence for wiki articles

The [GBIF service](../server/src/wiki/external-data/services/gbif.ts) fetches taxon metadata and occurrences against the Catalogue of Life eXtended Release (COL XR). Carry the checklist key and selected accepted taxon key with both responses. Occurrence searches accept a checklist key and otherwise default to the legacy Backbone. GBIF can change experimental endpoints without notice. [GBIF taxonomy interpretation](https://techdocs.gbif.org/en/data-processing/taxonomy-interpretation), [GBIF API reference](https://techdocs.gbif.org/en/openapi/)

The current decoder reads only `locationID` and `locality` from taxon distributions. The current plant fetcher's occurrence search is limited to 20 records with a still image. The sketch calls for retaining establishment means and source details, plus a separate occurrence query for geography. [GBIF response schemas](../server/src/wiki/external-data/services/gbif.schema.ts), [Plant fetcher](../server/src/wiki/external-data/kinds/plant-fetcher.ts)

## Distributions and geographical origin

- In a structured checklist distribution, `establishmentMeans: "NATIVE"` is explicit native-range evidence. `INTRODUCED` is a separate classification. Retain status, time and source details when supplied. For example, an explicitly native area with historical or extirpated status describes historical native range and gives no evidence of current presence. The checklist model separates establishment means from presence status, and the experimental endpoint includes structured native rows in practice. [GBIF distribution model](https://gbif.github.io/gbif-api/apidocs/org/gbif/api/model/checklistbank/Distribution.html), [GBIF checklist vocabulary](https://gbif.github.io/gbif-api/apidocs/org/gbif/api/vocabulary/EstablishmentMeans.html), [Acacia dealbata COL XR response](https://api.gbif.org/v2/experimental/taxon/7ddf754f-d193-4cc9-b351-99906754a03b/8P4G/info)

- The canvas indexes GBIF results and their fetch-time taxon selection in `GbifObservationsById`. Geographical Origin references the current taxon observation by ID and shows explicit native rows alongside localized contributor text. For *Acacia dealbata*, Tasmania appears in native range and Western Australia remains in the source observation as introduced. [Acacia dealbata COL XR response](https://api.gbif.org/v2/experimental/taxon/7ddf754f-d193-4cc9-b351-99906754a03b/8P4G/info)
- Leave establishment unknown when omitted. The live *Zea mays* response has structured `locationID` rows with `INTRODUCED` and others without a code. The *Coffea arabica* response instead has a single `locality` string containing a mix of places and inline qualifiers. Keep this text as distribution evidence. Turning it into origin entries would require a documented source-specific interpretation. [Zea mays COL XR response](https://api.gbif.org/v2/experimental/taxon/7ddf754f-d193-4cc9-b351-99906754a03b/5CXX5/info), [Coffea arabica COL XR response](https://api.gbif.org/v2/experimental/taxon/7ddf754f-d193-4cc9-b351-99906754a03b/WVWV/info)
- Interpret `locationID` in its own scheme, preserving its original value and label. TDWG botanical areas can differ in scope from political boundaries. Link only equivalent gazetteer places, and keep an area label and identifier when the match is missing or ambiguous. [TDWG plant distribution areas](https://www.tdwg.org/standards/wgsrpd/), [Kew WCVP distribution guidance](https://powo.science.kew.org/about-wcvp)

## Occurrences and place articles

- Occurrences are records of detections or non-detections at a place and time. Use `occurrenceStatus=DETECTED` for a sightings view. GBIF continues to accept the older `PRESENT` search filter, and historical observations can carry the old status values. Keep the occurrence key, dataset key, event date, basis of record and taxonomic match. `taxonKey` searches include synonyms and descendants, so inspect the classification for the chosen checklist before attributing records at a specific rank. Occurrences alone do not establish native range or a wild population. [GBIF status vocabulary source](https://gbif.github.io/gbif-api/apidocs/src-html/org/gbif/api/vocabulary/OccurrenceStatus.html), [GBIF occurrence search parameters](https://gbif.github.io/gbif-api/apidocs/org/gbif/api/model/occurrence/search/OccurrenceSearchParameter.html)
- Treat geography as graded by the evidence available. Use an explicit crosswalk for `countryCode` or GADM `gid`. GBIF can derive country from coordinates, and it attaches GADM identifiers independently of the publisher’s `stateProvince`. A spatial link needs valid coordinates and a place boundary that contains the reported footprint or uncertainty envelope. A point alone can be a grid centroid or generalized locality. Where flags, ambiguity or withheld/generalized data make a detailed assignment unreliable, retain a supported broader area or the unlinked evidence. A missing uncertainty radius leaves spatial uncertainty unknown. [GBIF geocoding](https://techdocs.gbif.org/en/data-processing/), [GBIF occurrence issues](https://techdocs.gbif.org/en/data-use/occurrence-issues-and-flags), [GBIF coordinate quality guidance](https://www.gbif.org/data-quality-requirements-occurrences), [Darwin Core terms](https://dwc.tdwg.org/list/)
- Use the existing Who’s On First gazetteer as the identity for `PLACE` articles. Its municipality lookup currently tests the center point. Add a footprint-aware check before using it for fine-grained occurrence links. Resolve and group the places selected for the article summary before calling `ensurePlaceArticle` to reuse articles by gazetteer or Wikidata identity. [Gazetteer lookup](../server/src/places/gazetteer.ts), [Place article service](../server/src/places/service.ts)
- Keep occurrence examples bounded and label them as a sample. A search can also return country and GADM facets for the same query, but GADM facets combine administrative levels, and both geography and taxonomic filters affect counts. Display these as matching records rather than population counts. GBIF offers map tiles for a map and asynchronous downloads for bulk analysis. [GBIF occurrence API](https://techdocs.gbif.org/en/openapi/v1/occurrence), [GBIF Maps API](https://techdocs.gbif.org/en/openapi/v2/maps), [GBIF occurrence downloads](https://techdocs.gbif.org/en/data-use/api-downloads)
