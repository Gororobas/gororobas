# Municipal boundary snapshots

The GeoJSON features are the research spike’s EPSG:4326 exports. Their filenames use the Who’s On First municipal identifier. Each feature retains its source and vintage.

- IBGE supplied the 2024 *Malha Municipal Digital* for Minas Gerais and Rio de Janeiro. [IBGE territorial meshes](https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias/organizacao-do-territorio/malhas-territoriais/15774-malhas.html)
- Instituto Geográfico Agustín Codazzi supplied the Villa de Leyva municipal polygon through its territorial limits service, retrieved on 12 November 2026 and exported to GeoJSON. [IGAC territorial limits](https://mapas2.igac.gov.co/server/rest/services/limites/limites/MapServer) · [IGAC open-data policy](https://www.igac.gov.co/datos-abiertos/) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
